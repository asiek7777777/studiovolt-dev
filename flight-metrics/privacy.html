Privacy Policy — Flight Metrics

Last updated: 2 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flight Metrics (the “App”) handles your data, what limited technical information our third-party service providers process on our behalf, and your rights under the EU GDPR, the Swiss revised Federal Act on Data Protection (revFADP/nFADP), and other applicable laws.

Data controller: Joanna Bednarczyk (independent developer based in Switzerland), operating under the trade name “Studio Volt”. Contact: contact@studiovolt.dev.

The Short Version

  • Your flight recordings (GPS routes, speed, altitude, G-force, barometric pressure) stay on your device only. We do not operate any servers that store flight data.
  • The App uses three third-party providers for crash reporting, subscription management, and map display. They process limited technical data (such as your IP address, device model, crash diagnostics, and anonymous purchase identifiers) — described in detail below.
  • No user accounts, no logins, no advertising, no behavioural tracking, no sale of data.
  • You can delete all locally stored data at any time by removing flights in the App or uninstalling the App.

1. Data That Stays on Your Device

Flight Metrics records flight data (GPS location, speed, heading, altitude, barometric pressure, G-force) using your device's built-in sensors. All recorded data is stored locally in an on-device SQLite database. We do not operate any servers that receive your flight data. There are no user accounts, no logins, and no cloud sync.

2. Data Processed by Third-Party Service Providers

The App relies on three third-party processors to function. Each receives only the minimum technical data described below, under a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) compliant with GDPR Art. 28. None of these providers receives your recorded flight data, name, or email address. Map-tile requests necessarily indicate which map areas your device displays or downloads (see Section 2.3).

2.1 Crash Reports & Technical Diagnostics — Sentry

In production builds only, the App sends two kinds of technical reports to Sentry’s EU-hosted infrastructure (de.sentry.io, primary processing in Germany): (a) crash and error reports, when the App crashes or encounters an error, and (b) a small number of predefined technical diagnostic events marking steps of key App flows — for example, the purchase screen being shown, or a purchase succeeding, failing, being cancelled, or restored. Each report contains:

  • Stack trace and error message (for crashes and errors) or a short, predefined event name (for diagnostic events)
  • Device model, iOS/Android version, App version
  • An anonymous, auto-generated Sentry install identifier (UUID)
  • Diagnostic breadcrumbs (e.g. which screen was open), with any location-like values removed before sending

We have configured Sentry not to store your IP address (server-side IP storage is disabled and IP-derived geolocation is scrubbed), and the SDK is set to not send personal data (sendDefaultPii off). Sentry acts as our processor under a GDPR Art. 28 DPA. Limited support and backup functions may involve Sentry sub-processors located outside the EU under Standard Contractual Clauses (see Section 9).

Purpose: detect and fix bugs, and understand where technical failures occur in flows such as the purchase flow.
Legal basis: legitimate interest in app stability and the correct functioning of the App (you may object — see “Your Rights” below).

2.2 Subscription Management — RevenueCat, Inc. (USA)

When you purchase, restore, or query a subscription, the following is sent to RevenueCat:

  • Anonymous App User ID (UUID per install — we never link it to your real identity)
  • Apple App Store receipt for the purchase
  • Device model, iOS/Android version, App version
  • Your IP address
  • Subscription/entitlement state, purchase and restore events

Purpose: validate subscriptions, prevent fraud, enable “Restore Purchases” across reinstalls.
Legal basis: performance of a contract.
Provider: RevenueCat is a US-based processor; transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses under RevenueCat’s DPA. See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy for details.

2.3 Map Tiles — Mapbox, Inc. (USA)

When you view the map, your device requests map tiles from Mapbox. Each request transmits:

  • Your IP address (used by Mapbox to deliver tiles and prevent abuse)
  • Device model, OS version, App version

If you use the offline-map corridor download, the destination name you type is sent to Mapbox’s geocoding service to locate it on the map; it is not stored by us and is not linked to your identity.

We have disabled Mapbox’s analytics/telemetry SDK in the App, so no Mapbox usage telemetry is sent from your device. Mapbox acts as our processor under a GDPR Art. 28 DPA; US transfers are covered by Mapbox’s EU-US Data Privacy Framework self-certification (verified active) and Standard Contractual Clauses. See Mapbox’s Privacy Policy.

2.4 Payment Processing — Apple Inc.

All subscription purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store. We never receive your credit card number, billing address, or full Apple ID. Apple's handling of payment data is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not use advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use behavioural analytics or marketing trackers (no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no AppsFlyer, no Adjust, no Amplitude, no Mixpanel). The technical diagnostic events described in Section 2.1 are used to diagnose whether core App flows work correctly — not to profile you, personalise content, or advertise.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites (no IDFA/ATT tracking).
  • We do not link the anonymous identifiers used by Sentry, RevenueCat, or Mapbox to your real-world identity.

4. Data Retention

  • Flight recordings & App settings: stored on your device until you delete them in the App or uninstall it.
  • Sentry crash and diagnostic data: up to 30 days.
  • RevenueCat subscription data: for the lifetime of the subscription plus the period required by tax and accounting law.
  • Mapbox tile request logs: controlled by Mapbox under their privacy policy.

5. Your Rights (GDPR / revFADP)

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we (or our processors) hold about you
  • Rectification of inaccurate data
  • Erasure (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Restriction of processing
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where based on consent)
  • Lodge a complaint with the Swiss FDPIC (edoeb.admin.ch) or your local Data Protection Authority.

To exercise any right, email contact@studiovolt.dev. We will respond within 30 days. Because flight recordings live only on your device, the most effective way to delete them is to remove them in the App or uninstall the App.

6. International Data Transfers

Flight data never leaves your device. For the limited technical data above: Sentry processing is primarily in the EU (de.sentry.io), with limited support/backup functions potentially involving SCC-covered sub-processors outside the EU. RevenueCat (USA) relies on Standard Contractual Clauses (it is not DPF-certified). Mapbox (USA) is DPF self-certified and additionally covered by SCCs. See each processor’s DPA/Privacy Policy in Sections 2 and 9.

7. Children

Flight Metrics is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the App, contact contact@studiovolt.dev.

8. Security

Flight data is stored locally on your device under your operating system’s built-in protections. Network communications with our service providers are encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and security cannot be guaranteed.

9. Sub-processors

Our processors engage their own sub-processors. Current lists: Sentry · RevenueCat · Mapbox.
