Larietta — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026 · Applies to the Larietta iOS app.

Polski: dostępne wkrótce.

1. Who is responsible

The data controller is Joanna Bednarczyk (independent developer), Switzerland, operating under the trade name “Studio Volt”. Contact for anything in this policy: contact@studiovolt.dev

2. The short version

  • Larietta works without an account — there is nothing to register, and no profile of you is created on our side.
  • Your location is processed on your device: trips are turned into map squares on your iPhone, and your location is not transmitted to us.
  • Voice notes are transcribed on your device and the audio is deleted immediately — it is never stored or uploaded.
  • If you turn on sync, it runs through your personal iCloud; we cannot access it.

3. Data that stays on your device

The following is processed and stored locally on your iPhone and is not transmitted to us:

  • Location and trips: while you record a trip, your location is used on the device to unlock map squares and show your position. The recording is processed into squares and trip statistics on the device, and the raw location trace is discarded when that processing completes;
  • voice notes: recorded notes are transcribed on the device to capture what you liked; the audio is deleted immediately after transcription and is never uploaded;
  • your preferences: what the app learns about the outings you enjoy is computed and kept on the device only;
  • your unlocked squares, saved outings, and settings.

You can erase all of it at any time in the app’s Settings, and deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

4. iCloud sync

If you enable sync, your squares, trips, and preferences are synced through your personal iCloud account, in your private iCloud storage, under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. We cannot access this data. You can manage or disable iCloud for Larietta in your device settings.

5. Data processed when you use online features

Maps and outing content. When you download or refresh maps and outing content for a region, the app requests that region’s content from our infrastructure. This request does not include your trips, squares, or preferences.

Reporting incorrect information. If you choose to report incorrect information (for example, wrong opening hours), the app sends the report — the place concerned and the issue. There are no accounts, and the report contains no name or contact details.

Technical data. Like any online service, our infrastructure briefly processes technical connection data (such as IP address) to deliver content and protect the service against abuse.

6. Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple under Apple’s own terms and privacy policy. To recognize your subscription, we process a purchase identifier provided by Apple and your subscription status. This contains no name, email address, or payment details.

7. Service providers

We use a small number of service providers, acting on our instructions under data processing agreements:

Provider What it does What it processes
Cloudflare service infrastructure (content delivery) the request data described in section 5

We use no analytics or advertising services in the app.

8. Legal bases

Where the GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), the UK GDPR, or the Swiss FADP applies, we process data:

  • to perform our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): delivering maps, outing content, and your subscription;
  • for our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, and reviewing reports to correct outing information.

9. Retention

  • Raw location traces: processed into squares on your device and discarded when processing completes; never transmitted to us.
  • Voice note audio: deleted immediately after on-device transcription; never transmitted to us.
  • Technical connection data: kept only as long as needed to deliver content and keep the service secure.
  • Reports of incorrect information: kept only as long as needed to review and correct the information concerned.
  • Subscription status: kept while needed to recognize your purchase.
  • On-device and iCloud data: under your control, until you delete it.

10. International transfers

Our infrastructure provider processes data in the United States and other countries. Where data is transferred from Switzerland, the EU/EEA, or the UK, safeguards recognized under applicable data protection law — in particular standard contractual clauses — are in place.

11. Your rights and controls

You do not need an account to exercise anything here:

  • Delete: erase all app data on your device in the app’s Settings; deleting the app removes all locally stored data. iCloud data is managed through your device’s iCloud settings.
  • You also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability. Write to contact@studiovolt.dev — we answer without undue delay.
  • You can lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority: in Switzerland the FDPIC; in the EU/EEA or UK, your local data protection authority.

12. Minors

Larietta is intended for users aged 16 or over (see the Terms of Service) and is used by adults to plan outings. The app has no child profiles and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

13. Changes

When the app’s data handling changes, this policy is updated and the date above is revised. The current version is always available at https://studiovolt.dev/larietta/privacy.html.
